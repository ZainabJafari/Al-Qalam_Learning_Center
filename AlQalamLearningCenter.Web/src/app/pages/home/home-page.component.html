About our school

A trusted place to study, grow, and feel safe

The school is a small rented learning space created for girls who want to keep studying. It is built to feel respectful, organized, and welcoming, with lessons that support both school knowledge and personal confidence.

Why this matters

Education should never be taken from girls

In Afghanistan, many girls face serious barriers to continuing school. Al Qalam Learning Center is a small but meaningful answer: a safe classroom where girls are reminded that their dreams, minds, and futures matter.

Every girl has the right to learn. Every lesson is a step toward confidence, dignity, and possibility.
