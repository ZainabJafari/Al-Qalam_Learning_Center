Our People

Meet the People Behind Al-Qalam

Al-Qalam was founded by three Afghan women brought together by a shared belief in education and in the potential of every girl.

Behind the center is a personal commitment to creating something meaningful and lasting. We work closely, support one another and carry a shared responsibility for the decisions, challenges and hopes that come with building Al-Qalam.

For us, this work is deeply personal. Every student who walks through Al-Qalam's doors is a reminder of why we began and why we remain committed to giving this work the care, dedication and responsibility it deserves.
